The Handmade Ritual Circle

A private Facebook Group and a short monthly letter for people who want to learn to make, care for, and give handmade things well. A classroom and a kitchen table, not a sales channel. Members hear about new pieces first, but that's the smallest part of it.

A weekly rhythm you can count on.

MondayMaterial Monday. One stone, bead, wax, fragrance note, metal, or care concept, explained in plain language.
WednesdayBehind the scenes. A short making video: pouring, stringing, cutting soap, packing orders, and the occasional failed burn test.

MonthlyLive beginner workshop. Make a bracelet with us, learn candle care, pick a gift, or ask a maker anything. Recorded for members who can't make it live.

What the Circle is, and isn't.

Teaching first

Most posts teach something. If a week goes by with more selling than teaching, we've drifted, and you're allowed to say so. The group exists so that people leave knowing more than when they arrived: how a candle should burn, what a treated stone is, how to tell a fair price from a lazy one.

Honest questions welcome

“Is this stone real?” “Why did my candle tunnel?” “Is this price fair?” Ask. Nobody is talked down to for not knowing yet.

No miracle claims

We talk about meaning and tradition warmly. Claims that a stone or candle heals, cures, or attracts money aren't allowed. Kindness and evidence, both.

One or two emails a month. That's it.

New pieces before anyone else, the Material Monday roundup, seasonal gift ideas, restock notices, and the workshop link. No daily launches. No fake urgency.

Join and we send the free guide, How to Care for Gemstone Jewelry and Burn Candles Safely, the same information we print on our care cards, in one place.

Prefer to just follow along? We post on Instagram, our one visual channel, and nowhere else.
