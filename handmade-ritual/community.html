Community

The Handmade Ritual Circle

A private Facebook Group and a short monthly letter for people who want to learn to make, care for, and give handmade things well. It is deliberately a classroom and a kitchen table, not a sales channel. Members do hear about new pieces first, but that's the smallest part of it.

What happens there

A weekly rhythm you can count on.

MondayMaterial Monday. One stone, bead, wax, fragrance note, metal, or care concept, explained in plain language.
WednesdayBehind the scenes. A short making video: pouring, stringing, cutting soap, packing orders, and the occasional failed burn test.
FridayMember spotlight. Your photos, a styling idea, or a simple candle ritual for the end of the week.
MonthlyLive beginner workshop. Make a bracelet with us, learn candle care, pick a gift, or ask a maker anything. Recorded for members who can't make it live.
QuarterlyDesign vote. Members choose the next colorway, stone combination, scent, or gift set. Voters get first access when it launches.
House rules

What the Circle is, and isn't.

Teaching first

Most posts teach something. If a week goes by with more selling than teaching, we've drifted, and you're allowed to say so.

Honest questions welcome

"Is this stone real?" "Why did my candle tunnel?" "Is this price fair?" Ask. Nobody gets talked down to for not knowing yet.

No miracle claims

We talk about meaning and tradition warmly. We don't allow claims that a stone or candle heals, cures, or attracts money. Kindness and evidence, both.

The letter

One or two emails a month. That's it.

New pieces before anyone else, the Material Monday roundup, seasonal gift ideas, restock notices, and the workshop link. No daily launches. No fake urgency.

Join and we'll send you the free guide, How to Care for Gemstone Jewelry and Burn Candles Safely: the same information we print on our care cards, in one place.

Prefer to just follow along? We post on Instagram, our one visual channel, and nowhere else.
