Plain-language policies.

The most frustrating thing about buying handmade is not knowing what happens when something goes wrong. Here is exactly what happens.

ShippingReturnsRepairs and resizingDamaged or lateCustom ordersCandle safetyDisclosuresPrivacy

Shipping

  • Processing time: 2-4 business days for in-stock items, updated in the announcement bar whenever it changes. Gift sets and custom pieces add about a week; we tell you the exact date before you pay.
  • Tracking is emailed for every order.
  • Candles ship in a padded box and are held during extreme heat so they don't arrive melted. If a heat hold applies to your order, you get an email the same day.
  • International: currently U.S. only. Ask us if you're elsewhere; we can usually work something out.

Returns and exchanges

  • Unused jewelry, unburned candles, and unopened soap can be returned within 30 days for a refund or exchange. Return postage is on us if we made a mistake, and on you if you changed your mind.
  • Bracelet doesn't fit? Skip the return. See resizing; it's free.
  • For hygiene reasons, opened soap isn't returnable, but if you dislike it, tell us why and we'll make it right anyway.

Repairs and resizing

  • Free resizing on any bracelet, any time in the first 60 days. After that, for the cost of postage.
  • Free restringing if a cord breaks in the first year. Elastic is a wear item; after a year, we restring for the cost of postage.
  • Necklace clasps and knots repaired free for life on anything we made.
  • Candle defects (tunneling on a correctly burned first burn, a wick that drowns or won't stay lit, a jar that cracks) are replaced with no return required. Send a photo.

Damaged in transit, or late

  • Arrived broken? Email a photo within 14 days and we ship a replacement immediately. You don't need to send the broken one back.
  • If your order will be late, you hear from us before the ship date, not after. A delay is forgivable; silence isn't, and we know it.
  • Tracking hasn't moved in 10 days? Tell us and we open a carrier claim and reship.

Custom orders

  • We take a small number of custom pieces each month: a stone combination, a scent for an event, a bulk order of gift sets.
  • You get a written description, a price, and a ship date before paying anything. Custom work is non-refundable once started, but we fix anything that doesn't match the description.

Candle safety

Every candle is tested in its own vessel with its own wick and fragrance load before it is sold. Every label carries the three core warnings (burn within sight, keep away from things that catch fire, keep away from children and pets) plus wick and burn-time instructions, following the ASTM F2058 fire-safety labeling standard. Full care guidance is on the Learn page.

If a candle ever behaves unsafely (a flame over an inch and a half, heavy soot, a jar too hot to hold), extinguish it and contact us. We replace it and log the report.

Gemstone and product-claims disclosure

  • Every stone we sell is described with its treatment status: natural and untreated, heated, dyed, stabilized, coated, lab-created, or cultured. We follow the FTC Jewelry Guides on disclosure and state it even when not required.
  • We never sell imitation stones described as gemstones, or plated metal described as gold.
  • Our jewelry, candles, and soap are for personal enjoyment, ritual, decor, and gifting. They are not medical products. We make no claim that they diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, or that they change health, wealth, relationships, or mental-health outcomes. Any meanings we mention are traditional associations, offered as an invitation for reflection.
  • Soap ingredient lists follow cosmetic labeling requirements and are printed on every wrapper.
