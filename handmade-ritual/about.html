Made by one pair of hands, slowly on purpose.

Ember and Stone started at a kitchen table with a bag of rose quartz beads and a candle that tunneled. Fixing that candle took six weeks and a notebook. That notebook is still how everything here gets made.

Hello. I'm the person who makes these.

Replace this with your real story, in your own voice, next to a real photo. Customers want to know who made the piece, why it exists, and how. It's the single biggest advantage a small maker has over a warehouse.

I started making bracelets for friends going through hard seasons, a reminder on the wrist that someone was thinking of them. Candles came next, because a gift wanted a second half. Soap came because I wanted a whole box you could give without adding anything.

I make everything myself. When something is out of stock, it's because I haven't made it yet, not because a warehouse is empty.

The unglamorous parts are the important parts.

Candles

Every new wax, fragrance, wick, and vessel combination gets its own testing log: fragrance percentage, cure time, flame height, melt pool, soot, jar temperature, and the last inch of the burn. Nothing is sold, or given away, until the result is safe and repeats three times. Wax is a coconut-soy blend, wicks are cotton and sized per jar, fragrance is always under the maximum safe load.

Jewelry and beads

Beads come from suppliers who state the stone and any treatment in writing, and we pass that on. Bracelets are double-strung on fresh elastic and pull-tested. Necklaces are hand-knotted. Glass beads are made at the torch and kiln-annealed so they don't crack. Metals are sterling silver, 14k gold-filled, and raw brass. Never plated sold as gold.

Soap

Cold-process, palm-free recipes, poured in small loaves and hand-cut. Bars cure for six weeks on racks before wrapping so they're hard, mild, and long-lasting. Every wrapper lists every ingredient by weight. Scented with essential oils only; an unscented bar is always available.

Ten things you can hold us to.

  1. Quality that matches the price, or your money back.
  2. Photos of the actual item, unfiltered, with something for scale.
  3. Every listing answers the six questions before you have to ask.
  4. Every stone's treatment status stated plainly.
  5. Every candle burn-tested in its own jar before sale.
  6. Replies within one business day, usually faster.
  7. An honest processing time, and a message before we miss it.
  8. A written policy for damage, sizing, repairs, and late orders.
  9. No health, wealth, or healing claims. Ever.
  10. More teaching than selling, in the group and in the letter.
