About

Made by one pair of hands, in small batches, slowly on purpose.

Ember & Stone started at a kitchen table with a bag of rose quartz beads and a candle that tunneled. Fixing that candle took six weeks and a notebook. That notebook is still how everything here gets made.

How things are made

The unglamorous parts are the important parts.

Candles

Every new wax, fragrance, wick, and vessel combination gets its own testing log: fragrance percentage, cure time, flame height, melt pool, soot, jar temperature, and the last inch of the burn. Nothing is sold, or given away, until the result is safe and repeats three times.

Wax: coconut-soy blend. Wicks: cotton, sized per jar. Fragrance: phthalate-free oils and essential oils, always under the maximum safe load.

Jewelry & beads

Beads are bought from suppliers who state the stone and any treatment in writing, and we pass that on. Bracelets are double-strung on fresh elastic and pull-tested. Necklaces are hand-knotted so a break loses one pearl, not a strand. Glass beads are made at the torch and kiln-annealed so they don't crack.

Metals: sterling silver, 14k gold-filled, and raw brass. Never plated sold as gold.

Soap

Cold-process, palm-free recipes, poured in small loaves and hand-cut. Bars cure for six weeks on racks before wrapping so they're hard, mild, and long-lasting. Every wrapper lists every ingredient by weight.

Scented with essential oils only. Unscented options always available.
