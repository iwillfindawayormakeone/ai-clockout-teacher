The product rule

Six questions every listing answers before you have to ask.

The most common complaint about handmade shops is a vague listing. So every item here answers these six, in the shop and on the label.

  1. What exactly is it?Type, style, and what's in the box.
  2. What is it made from?Wax, wick, metal, stone, oils, and any treatments.
  3. How big is it?Weight, dimensions, burn time, or wrist size.
  4. How do I use and care for it?Wick trimming, cord care, soap drying.
  5. When will it ship?An honest processing time, plus tracking.
  6. What if there's a problem?Replace, repair, resize, or refund. In writing.

Why a candle takes six weeks to become a product.

Every new scent gets a testing log. If it tunnels, smokes, or runs too hot, it doesn't get sold. It gets fixed.

Test

Wax, fragrance percentage, wick size, vessel, cure time, flame height, melt pool, soot, jar temperature, and the last inch of the burn.

Pull

Bracelets are double-strung and every cord and clasp is pull-tested before it's packed. Necklaces are knotted so a break loses one pearl, not a strand.

Wait

Soap cures on racks for six full weeks so it's hard, mild, and long-lasting. This is slower. It is also the whole point.
