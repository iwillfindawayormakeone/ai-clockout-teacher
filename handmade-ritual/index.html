The product rule

Six questions every listing answers before you have to ask.

The most common complaint about handmade shops is a vague listing. So every item here, in the shop and on the label, answers these six things.

What exactly is it?Type, style, and what's in the box.
What is it made from?Wax, wick, metal, stone, oils, and any treatments.
How big is it?Weight, dimensions, burn time, or wrist size.
How do I use or care for it?Wick trimming, cord care, soap drying.
When will it ship?An honest processing time plus tracking.
What if there's a problem?Replace, repair, resize, or refund. In writing.

From the studio

Why a candle takes six weeks to become a product.

Every new scent gets a testing log: wax, fragrance percentage, wick size, vessel, cure time, flame height, melt pool, soot, jar temperature, and how it behaves at the very end of the burn. If it tunnels, smokes, or runs too hot, it doesn't get sold. It gets fixed.

Bracelets are double-strung and every clasp and cord is pulled before it's packed. Soap cures for six full weeks so it's hard, mild, and long-lasting. This is slower. It is also the whole point.
