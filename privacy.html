This site exists to do one thing: register you for the free Leave at the Bell live training. This policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect when you do that and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • First name and email address — when you register for the training.
  • Cell phone number (optional) — only if you choose to add it on the thank-you page to receive text reminders.

That's it. This site uses no analytics trackers, no advertising pixels, and no cookies. (Your browser briefly holds your name and email in session storage between the registration page and the thank-you page so you don't have to type them twice; it never leaves your device that way.)

How we use it

  • To send your registration confirmation, your seat link, and reminder emails for the training you signed up for.
  • If you gave a phone number: to send at most a few reminder texts about this training. Never marketing blasts.
  • To occasionally send teaching-related emails from AI Clock Out Teacher after the training. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

What we never do

We never sell, rent, or share your email or phone number with anyone. Full stop.

Who processes it for us

  • Brevo (our email/SMS platform) stores your contact details and sends the confirmation, reminders, and texts. See Brevo's privacy policy.
  • The live training itself is hosted on a video platform (e.g. Zoom or Google Meet); joining it is governed by that platform's own policy.

Opting out

  • Email: click "unsubscribe" at the bottom of any email, any time.
  • Texts: reply STOP to any text.
  • Everything: email us (below) and we'll delete your contact record entirely.

Children

This site and training are for adults (teachers and educators). We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.
