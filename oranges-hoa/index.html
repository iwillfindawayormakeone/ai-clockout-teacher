Pay my dues

One assessment per household each year, invoiced by mail. The amount is on your invoice and is due within 30 days of the invoice date.

By check, no fee

Pay to the order ofThe Orange’s Homeowners Association, Inc.
AmountAs shown on your invoice

Memo line: your street address, so the treasurer can credit the right home.

Online

Prefer to skip the checkbook? Pay online and the money goes straight to the association’s account. A small processing fee applies only to online payments. Paying by check stays free.

Online payment coming soon

Reminders, not surprises

Every household gets a friendly email before the due date and again before any late fee would apply. Nobody should learn about a deadline after it has passed.

Question about your balance?

A neighbor answers, not a call center. Email the board and you’ll hear back within a few days.

From the Bylaws (Article XI): assessments unpaid 15 days after the due date incur a late fee of $15 or one-tenth of the delinquent amount (whichever is greater), plus interest at 6% per year. Unpaid assessments are secured by a lien on the property. Members more than 60 days delinquent may not vote or serve on the Board. If paying will be a hardship, contact the board early. Talking to us is always better than a late fee.

The rules, in plain words

Highlights from our governing documents. The full Declaration and Bylaws in documents always control.

§1

Vehicles & parking

  • No vehicle repair on association property.
  • No RVs, boats, trailers, campers, or similar vehicles parked or stored without written Board consent.
  • Unlicensed or inoperable vehicles may stay no more than 72 hours; after 10 days’ written notice they may be towed at the owner’s expense.
§2

Renting your home

  • Leases must be written, residential-only, and for the whole home. No room rentals.
  • Minimum lease is one year unless the Board grants a short-term waiver.
  • A Montgomery County rental license is required; give the Board a copy on request, with tenant names and your current address.
§3

Exterior changes

  • Exterior modifications need Architectural Control Committee review before work begins. Ask here.
  • When in doubt, ask first. A quick email saves everyone time and money.

How the association runs

Five-member Board

Directors serve staggered three-year terms with no pay. Officers are elected by the Board each year.

Annual meeting each December

Written or electronic notice at least 15 days ahead. Quorum is 40% of votes; one vote per lot, in person or by proxy.

Monthly board meetings

With reasonable notice to members. Minutes are kept by the Secretary and posted in documents.

Independent annual audit

Finances are audited each year by an independent accountant and presented at the annual meeting.

Open records

Books and records are open to member inspection under the Maryland Homeowners Association Act.

Who fixes what

Some things are the county’s or the utility’s job, and going straight to them is faster. Report it to us too and we will follow up.

  • Pothole, street sign, storm drain: Montgomery County 311 (or dial 311)
  • Streetlight out: Pepco’s streetlight outage form, with the pole number if you can read it
  • Trash or recycling missed, holiday schedule: County Solid Waste Services
  • Downed wire: stay back and call 911 if it could be a power line; cable or phone lines go to the provider
  • Anything inside the association: the button above
